2. Hosting and server logs

GitHub Pages hosts both sites. The provider is GitHub Inc., 88 Colin P. Kelly Jr. Street, San Francisco, CA 94107, USA, a subsidiary of Microsoft Corporation.

When you open a page, your browser sends data to the servers of GitHub. GitHub records this data in server logs:

  • your IP address,
  • the date and time of the request,
  • the requested page,
  • the referring page (referrer),
  • the browser and the operating system (user agent).

Purpose: to deliver the pages, and to keep the service stable and secure. Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR. Our legitimate interest is a reliable and secure website. GitHub keeps the logs as its privacy statement describes. We do not have access to these logs.

GitHub can process data in the USA. GitHub Inc. is certified under the EU-US Data Privacy Framework. The adequacy decision of the European Commission (Art. 45 GDPR) applies to this transfer.

3. No cookies, no tracking, no external content

The sites set no cookies. They use no analytics and no tracking. All fonts, scripts and images come from the sites themselves. Your browser does not connect to third parties when you read the pages.

4. Storage in your browser (metal.platform-kit.org)

metal.platform-kit.org stores some settings in your browser:

  • the color theme that you select, and the tab that you select in a group of tabs (local storage),
  • the open and closed groups of the navigation (session storage; your browser deletes it when you close the tab).

These settings do not leave your device. This storage is necessary for the functions that you request (§ 25(2) no. 2 TDDDG). You can delete it in the settings of your browser.

The search on metal.platform-kit.org runs in your browser. Your browser loads the search index from the site. Your search terms do not go to a server.

5. Contact by email

When you send an email, we process your address and your message to reply to you. Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR, or Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR if your message is about a contract. We delete the data when we no longer need it and no law requires us to keep it.

6. Links to other sites

The sites link to other sites, for example to GitHub. When you follow such a link, the privacy policy of that site applies.

7. Your rights

You have these rights for your personal data:

  • access (Art. 15 GDPR),
  • rectification (Art. 16 GDPR),
  • erasure (Art. 17 GDPR),
  • restriction of processing (Art. 18 GDPR),
  • data portability (Art. 20 GDPR),
  • objection to processing based on Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR, for reasons that come from your particular situation (Art. 21 GDPR).

To use these rights, send an email to the controller. You also have the right to complain to a data protection supervisory authority (Art. 77 GDPR), for example the authority of the EU country where you live.

8. Changes

We update this policy when the sites or the law change. Version: November 2026.
